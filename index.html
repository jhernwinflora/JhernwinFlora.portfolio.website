Overview & Business Problem

Retail management required clear oversight of historical product sales trends, customer purchase frequencies, and revenue margins across various product lines. Uncleaned transactional records made executive decision-making difficult.

Objectives

  • Standardize and clean raw retail transaction tables using MySQL Workbench.
  • Identify top-performing product categories, regional drivers, and seasonal sales trends.
  • Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for executive reporting and time-series evaluation.

Dataset Description

Primary dataset: retail_sales_data.csv. Captures 20,000 unique orders across 3,985 unique customers from 2023 to 2025. Key fields include order metrics (order_id, order_date), customer profiling (customer_id, gender, age), regional dimensions (region, city), product catalog details (category, product), and financial records (unit_price, quantity, discount, sales, profit).

Data Cleaning & SQL Workflows

Utilized MySQL Workbench to resolve missing values, standardize date strings to ISO format, and write advanced aggregation and window queries:

1. Date Standardization & Schema Alteration:

UPDATE sales_data
SET order_date = STR_TO_DATE(order_date, "%m/%d/%Y");

ALTER TABLE sales_data
MODIFY COLUMN order_date DATE;

2. Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth Analysis:

WITH year_month_sales AS (
    SELECT
        YEAR(order_date) AS year,
        MONTH(order_date) AS month_num,
        MONTHNAME(order_date) AS monthname,
        SUM(sales) AS monthly_sales
    FROM sales_data
    GROUP BY year, month_num, monthname
)
SELECT
    year, monthname, monthly_sales,
    LAG(monthly_sales, 1) OVER(ORDER BY year ASC, month_num ASC) AS prev_monthly_sales,
    ROUND((monthly_sales - LAG(monthly_sales, 1) OVER(ORDER BY year ASC, month_num ASC)) 
    / LAG(monthly_sales, 1) OVER(ORDER BY year ASC, month_num ASC) * 100, 2) AS mom_growth_pct
FROM year_month_sales
ORDER BY year ASC, month_num ASC;

Power BI Dashboard Preview

The dashboard tracks key regional performance metrics, monthly growth trends, and product category breakdowns.

Key Findings

  • Q4 Peak Sales: Revenue consistently peaked across October and November (reaching $10.64M in Nov 2025).
  • Revenue vs. Volume Divergence: Laptops dominated total revenue ($130.72M) despite ranking #3 in volume, whereas Wireless Earbuds led in unit sales (2,606 units) but ranked #6 in total revenue.
  • Category Concentration: Electronics generated $186.89M, significantly outperforming all other categories combined.
  • Regional Performance: The South region led overall sales ($70.07M), while the East region lagged behind at $36.89M.

Business Recommendations

  • Optimize inventory buffer levels and marketing spend starting late Q3 (September) to capture peak Q4 demand surges.
  • Implement cross-selling bundles pairing top revenue drivers (Laptops) with high-volume accessories (Wireless Earbuds) to push AOV beyond $11.10K.
  • Re-evaluate carrying costs for lower-yielding categories like Books & Stationery ($2.77M) and Beauty ($3.83M).

Overview & Business Problem

Customer acquisition cost in the banking sector is significantly higher than customer retention. Leadership identified an escalating attrition trend where profitable accounts were closing unexpectedly, resulting in an overall customer churn rate of 20.37% (2,037 out of 10,000 customers).

Objectives

  • Calculate baseline churn rates and quantify financial losses across account balances and salaries.
  • Evaluate regional performance across France, Germany, and Spain to determine geographic attrition drivers.
  • Analyze product adoption thresholds to understand why customers holding multiple products are leaving.
  • Investigate demographic risk factors including age cohorts, gender distributions, and credit score tiers.

Dataset Description

The dataset comprises 10,000 customer records capturing core banking attributes. Key fields include demographics (Geography, Gender, Age), banking profile (CreditScore, Tenure, Balance, NumOfProducts), engagement indicators (HasCrCard, IsActiveMember, EstimatedSalary), and the target variable Exited.

Data Cleaning & SQL Workflows

Utilized MySQL Workbench to stage data, standardize binary integer flags to categorical text, and execute exploratory aggregation queries:

1. Schema Modification & Value Mapping:

-- Standardize binary flags to categorical text
ALTER TABLE bank_churn
MODIFY IsActiveMember VARCHAR(10),
MODIFY HasCrCard VARCHAR(10);

UPDATE bank_churn
SET IsActiveMember = CASE WHEN IsActiveMember = '1' THEN 'Yes' ELSE 'No' END,
    HasCrCard = CASE WHEN HasCrCard = '1' THEN 'Yes' ELSE 'No' END;

2. Overall Churn Rate & Regional Analysis Queries:

-- Overall Churn Rate Calculation
SELECT
    COUNT(*) AS total_customers,
    SUM(CASE WHEN Exited = 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS churned_customers,
    ROUND((SUM(CASE WHEN Exited = 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) * 100.0) / COUNT(*), 2) AS churn_rate_pct
FROM bank_churn;

-- Regional Churn Breakdown
SELECT
    Geography,
    COUNT(*) AS total_customers,
    SUM(CASE WHEN Exited = 1 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS churned_customers,
    ROUND(AVG(Exited) * 100, 2) AS churn_rate_pct
FROM bank_churn
GROUP BY Geography
ORDER BY churn_rate_pct DESC;

Power BI Dashboard Preview

The dashboard tracks key regional performance metrics, customer churn distributions across age groups, balance brackets, and product adoption rates.

Key Findings

  • Germany Outlier Risk: Despite equal customer volume to Spain (~2.5K), Germany accounts for 814 churned customers (32.44% churn rate), nearly double France and Spain.
  • Product Over-Concentration Trap: Customers holding 3 products exhibit an alarming 82.71% churn rate, while those with 4 products experience 100% churn.
  • Age Cohort Vulnerability: Attrition peaks sharply in the 50–59 age bracket, reaching 62.95% for females and 49.55% for males.
  • Activity Status Protection: Inactive members account for the vast majority of customer attrition, confirming that active engagement significantly mitigates risk .

Strategic Business Recommendations

  • Deploy German Market Intervention: Investigate localized customer service quality, competitive product offerings, and fee structures in Germany to reverse the 32.4% attrition spike[cite: 6].
  • Restructure Multi-Product Bundles: Audit advanced products (products 3 and 4) to resolve usability friction or unexpected fee disclosures leading to massive drop-offs[cite: 6].
  • Senior Client Retention Taskforce: Establish dedicated relationship management for the high-risk 50–59 age demographic with personalized financial advisory services[cite: 6].
  • Automated Re-engagement Triggers: Set up CRM alerts for customers who become inactive for >90 days to deliver targeted incentives before account closure[cite: 6].

Overview & Business Problem

E-commerce retail organizations generate massive transactional datasets daily, but struggle to transform raw order logs into actionable commercial strategies. This project delivers an end-to-end data analytics solution for an Indian e-commerce retailer tracking nationwide sales across multiple states to evaluate revenue realization, dissect customer tier profitability, audit product category performance, and analyze fulfillment efficiency.

Objectives

  • Revenue Realization: Analyze gross potential revenue vs. realized revenue across order lifecycle stages (Delivered, Returned, Cancelled, Processing, Shipped).
  • Customer Segmentation: Evaluate how customer tiers (Platinum, Gold, Silver) and age demographics contribute to total sales volume.
  • Product & Discount Effectiveness: Identify top revenue-generating categories and brands while measuring how discount brackets influence purchasing behavior.
  • Logistics & Operations: Audit geographic sales distribution and order delivery speed tiers.

Dataset Description

The analysis utilizes three normalized CSV datasets:

  • customers.csv (40,000 rows): Customer demographics, registration dates, tiers, and lifetime spend.
  • products.csv (2,000 rows): Product catalog details, original prices, selling prices, discount percentages, stock quantities, and ratings.
  • sales.csv (250,000 rows): Transaction-level logs capturing order dates, delivery dates, quantities, unit prices, shipping costs, coupon details, payment modes, and order statuses.

Data Cleaning & SQL Workflows

Utilized MySQL Workbench to perform staging ingestion, schema alteration, date sanitization, and complex aggregation queries:

1. Schema Modification & Date Formatting:

ALTER TABLE customers
MODIFY COLUMN Date_of_Birth DATE,
MODIFY COLUMN Registration_Date DATE;

UPDATE sales
SET Order_Date = STR_TO_DATE(Order_Date, "%m/%d/%Y"),
    Delivery_Date = STR_TO_DATE(Delivery_Date, "%m/%d/%Y");

ALTER TABLE sales
MODIFY COLUMN Order_Date DATE,
MODIFY COLUMN Delivery_Date DATE;

2. Executive Overview & Lifecycle Revenue Realization Query:

SELECT 
    SUM(CASE WHEN Order_Status = 'Delivered' THEN Total_Amount ELSE 0 END) AS total_sales,
    SUM(CASE WHEN Order_Status = 'Returned' THEN Total_Amount ELSE 0 END) AS refunded_sales,
    SUM(CASE WHEN Order_Status = 'Cancelled' THEN Total_Amount ELSE 0 END) AS lost_sales,
    SUM(CASE WHEN Order_Status = 'Processing' THEN Total_Amount ELSE 0 END) AS processing_sales,
    SUM(CASE WHEN Order_Status = 'Shipped' THEN Total_Amount ELSE 0 END) AS pending_sales,
    SUM(Total_Amount) AS gross_potential_sales
FROM sales;

Key Findings & Insights

  • Revenue Realization & Leakage: Realized revenue stands at ₹4.74bn across delivered orders, while non-realized revenue highlights friction: ₹587M Pending, ₹300M Refunded, and ₹302M Lost revenue.
  • Electronics Monopoly: Electronics leads all categories by a massive margin, driving ₹3.44bn (72.62%) of total revenue.
  • Customer Tier Dominance: Platinum tier customers generate the overwhelming majority of revenue, heavily outperforming Gold and Silver tiers across standard payment modes.
  • Fulfillment & Discount Dynamics: Delivery speeds are split evenly across thirds (Fast 33.46%, Standard 33.39%, Slow 33.16%), while the 26%–50% discount bracket drives the highest revenue (₹2.3bn).

Strategic Business Recommendations

  • Mitigate Revenue Leakage: Address ₹587M in Pending and ₹302M in Lost revenue by optimizing checkout verification, reducing gateway drop-offs, and proactively communicating with customers.
  • Optimize High-Value Fulfillment: Streamline warehousing in top demand hubs (UP, Haryana, Rajasthan) to eliminate bottlenecks causing 33.16% of deliveries to take 6+ days.
  • Refine Discount Strategies: While the 26–50% bracket drives top-line growth (₹2.3bn), introduce targeted cross-selling and loyalty perks for Platinum members to protect profit margins from heavy markdowns .
  • Diversify Category Growth: Cross-promote high-margin accessories in underperforming categories (Home, Fashion, Beauty) to reduce over-reliance on consumer electronics.
